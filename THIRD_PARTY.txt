LiteRec bundles FFmpeg (ffmpeg.exe) for video encoding.

FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU GPL v3 for this build (see ffmpeg-LICENSE.txt).
Build provided by gyan.dev: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
FFmpeg project and source code: https://ffmpeg.org/ and https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg
The x264 encoder is included in that build: https://www.videolan.org/developers/x264.html
